Got Questions?

How do I book a session?

Simply fill out my contact form, send me an email, or message me on social media! Once we choose a date and location, I'll send over all the details to make your session stress-free.

What types of photography do you offer?

I currently offer seniors, couples, engagements, maternity, and select weddings.

Where are you located?

I'm based in West Michigan and serve the surrounding areas. I'm always open to traveling for sessions and special events. Travel fees may apply depending on the distance.

How long does it take to receive my photos?

Each session is different! Typical portrait sessions are delivered within 2–3 weeks. Wedding galleries may take longer with a 1-3 month return.

How will I receive my photos?

You'll receive an online gallery where you can view, download, and share your images with family and friends.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If weather impacts your outdoor session, we'll work together to reschedule for the next available date.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a non-refundable retainer is required to secure your date. The remaining balance is due before your session or event.

What is your editing style?

My editing style is light and airy. I want them to feel dreamy, clean, and timeless.
